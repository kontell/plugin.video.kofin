## 2. Cross-cutting: what no single package can see

### 2.1 Three plugin modules are service code, and a URL builder lives in the wrong layer

`service/` imports `kofin.plugin` at seven sites (`main.py:628, :854`, `settings_apply.py:212`, `player.py:1570, :1693`, `latesubs.py:47`, `remote.py:17`) and `syncplay/playback.py:564` makes an eighth. What they want is never a route. `plugin/adduser.py:146-359` (`detach_all`, `restore_additional_users`, `_publish_from_server`, `show_picker`) runs in the service and says so in its docstring (`:18-21`); only `:254-274` and `:362-434` are routes. `plugin/skip.py` (the skip overlay) has one caller, `service/player.py:1626`. `plugin/subtitles.fetch_to` (`:267-278`) is "the service's way in". And `listitems.plugin_url` (`plugin/listitems.py:65-66`) is imported by two service modules and `syncplay/playback.py` while `sync/nodes/video.py:271, :276` and `core/state.py:100` hand-build the same `plugin://plugin.video.kofin/?…` string — four builders in three packages. The one edge the other way, `plugin/play.py:357` → `service.segments.parse_segments`, is a pure parser. The cycle is real (`service.latesubs → plugin.subtitles`, `plugin.play → service.segments`) and harmless only because both ends are leaves.

The tree carries 69 function-local cross-package imports outside `sync/` (`plugin/actions.py` → `kofin.downloads` ×9, `service/player.py` → `kofin.downloads` ×6, `service/settings_apply.py` → `kofin.sync` ×5, …). Some are the documented "contained subsystem" reason (`service/main.py:459-462`); most are cycle-dodging, and `downloads`↔`sync` is bidirectional on both sides by that means (`sync/hooks.py:77-110`, `library.py:27`, `playlists.py:209`, `nodes/video.py:249, :293`, `nodes/music.py:89`, `clean.py:164, :195-196` one way; `downloads/manager.py:637-638` the other).

### 2.2 The manager and client seams are untyped

`Service.library`, `Service.downloads` and `Service.syncplay` are `Optional[Any]` (`service/main.py:160-162`). Across the three, 32 member accesses are checked by nobody: `library.is_alive` ×5, `enqueue_command` ×4, `startup_done` ×3, `stop_client` ×2, `stop_thread`, `workers_alive`, `refresh_libraries`, `added/updated/removed/userdata`; `downloads.submit/cancel/remove/remove_all/wake/stop`; `syncplay.on_notification/refresh_tempo_session/stop`, plus `on_wake/on_sleep/on_kodi_play` reached by name through `_syncplay_forward` (`main.py:1140-1147`) — the same seven lines as `Player._syncplay_event` (`player.py:684-692`). `SettingsApplier` takes `service: object` (`settings_apply.py:56`) and reaches nine members through seven `type: ignore[attr-defined]` and four `getattr`. `syncplay/manager.py:172, :191` call the REST client as `getattr(api_client, name)(*args)` from 17 string literals (26 verb names in all), which is why 21 `Api` methods have zero static callers and a rename in `core/api.py` passes mypy. `player.syncplay`, `remote.syncplay` (`main.py:572-573, :584-585`) and `player.syncplay_group_active` (`syncplay/manager.py:746, :801`) are attached after construction.

The `Library` port `Service` uses is 11 names. Phase 2 named exactly that port for `FullSync` in `tests/unit/synchost.py`; `test_service.py` references it zero times and carries seven hand-rolled `*Library` fakes instead (`:77, :88, :858, :927, :1175, :1272, :1809`).

The three `test_downloads_manager.py` failures the sync assessment recorded as flaky are not order-dependent. `files.free_space_ok` (`downloads/files.py:206-216`) runs a real `os.statvfs` on `tmp_path` with a 2 GiB reserve (`:33`); this host's `/tmp` is a 3.0 GB tmpfs with about 1.2 GB free.
